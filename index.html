SETUP, ONE TIME

Let Claude prepare your machine

Open Claude Code and paste this:

Check whether ffmpeg, node, python3 with pillow and numpy, and whisper-cpp with a base.en model are installed on this machine. Install anything that is missing, then confirm each one works.

It checks, installs what is missing, and tells you when it is done.

Connect Genrupt

Create your account at genrupt.com and add credits. Then connect Genrupt to Claude from Genrupt's own settings, under integrations. That link is what lets the skill render on your behalf.

Install the skill

Send me your GitHub username, accept the invite that arrives by email, then run:

npx skills add JayGPTPro/asin-to-video-autopilot -g

If it asks you to sign in to GitHub, say yes.

Confirm you are ready

In Claude Code:

Run the asin-to-video-autopilot environment check

One line per requirement. Anything not ready comes with its exact fix, and you can just tell Claude to handle it.

Make your first video

/asin-to-video-autopilot B0XXXXXXXXX

The first run asks one question, once: your spending limit per video. After that it works on its own and reports each cost as it happens. Fifteen to twenty-five minutes later the video is done.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your product stays your product

The render is locked to clean plates cut from your own listing images. No invented claims, no generated text in the frame, and never a fake logo: your real label or nothing.

It checks its own work

Every shot is measured against the plan for light, movement and continuity. That is how it catches the flaws that usually make AI video look like AI video.

People in the frame

Faces appear in the film, generated to match your product's buyer. Your own listing photos of people are screened out first, since the providers reject them.

Format

1280x720 widescreen, 27 to 30 seconds, English voiceover. The Amazon listing and Sponsored Brands format. Vertical cuts come later.
