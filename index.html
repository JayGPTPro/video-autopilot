CLAUDE CODE SKILL

Video Autopilot

One Amazon ASIN in. A finished 30-second listing video out: footage, sound design, music, voiceover and animated text. No questions during the run.

Made start to finish by the skill. One ASIN, no human edits.

WHAT YOU NEED

1

Claude Code. The desktop app or the CLI, either works.

2

A Genrupt account with credits. That is what renders the video and generates the music and voiceover. A small credit pack covers your first videos.

That is your whole list. The skill also uses a few free tools on your computer for editing and quality checks. You do not install them by hand: Claude does it for you in step one below.

SETUP, ONE TIME

Let Claude prepare your machine

Open Claude Code and paste this:

Check whether ffmpeg, node, python3 with pillow and numpy, and whisper-cpp with a base.en model are installed on this machine. Install anything that is missing, then confirm each one works.

It checks, installs what is missing, and tells you when it is done.

Connect Genrupt

Create your account at genrupt.com and add credits. Then connect Genrupt to Claude from Genrupt's own settings, under integrations. That link is what lets the skill render on your behalf.

Install the skill

Send me your GitHub username, accept the invite that arrives by email, then run:

npx skills add JayGPTPro/asin-to-video-autopilot -g

If it asks you to sign in to GitHub, say yes.

Confirm you are ready

In Claude Code:

Run the asin-to-video-autopilot environment check

One line per requirement. Anything not ready comes with its exact fix, and you can just tell Claude to handle it.

Make your first video

/asin-to-video-autopilot B0XXXXXXXXX

The first run asks one question, once: your spending limit per video. After that it works on its own and reports each cost as it happens. Fifteen to twenty-five minutes later the video is done.

WHAT HAPPENS IN A RUN

ResearchReads your listing and studies every image, and screens out the ones AI providers reject
DirectionWrites the film: hook, hero shot, four use cases, a close that answers the opening
Test shotRenders four seconds of the riskiest shot first and measures it
The filmThe full 30 seconds with its own sound effects
Self-checkMeasures every shot against the plan and repairs one flaw on its own if needed
AudioMusic options and a scripted voiceover, mixed under the film's own sound
FinishAnimated text, final checks, and a report of every decision it made

You set a spending limit once, and the skill enforces it: it reports what each step costs as it happens and stops before crossing your limit.
